# What we ask of people

*The spine of the ITP app, in one page. Agreed with Aaron, 15 Nov 2026. Everything on Today follows from this.*

## The sentence

**Live with one ITP at a time.**

Not "use an app with many kinds of content." Carry one piece of possibility for a stretch of days — a Universal, or your own — and let it get into you by three routes: the ear, the breath, and the day. You stay with it until you feel like changing, which is how people used the old app. Now and then you'll want to listen to something else for a moment; that's allowed, and it doesn't change what you're living with.

## One thing at three sizes

An ITP is not three pieces of content. It is one transmission at three sizes, and the size you reach for depends on the moment:

- **The attunement** — the whole transmission. Three minutes, eyes closed, the light. Morning, and whenever you want to go all the way in.
- **Its Breathe 11** — the same transmission condensed into the body. Eleven lines from the attunement, one per breath, two minutes. For when you have two minutes and not three, or when you're wound up and listening isn't possible but breathing is. *(Breathe 11s never stand alone; they belong to an attunement.)*
- **Its Quantum Why** — the same transmission reduced to one sentence that can land in an ordinary afternoon and tilt it. Ten seconds. It isn't a reminder; it's a gift. *"Why is the step so warm at five o'clock?"*

Primers are different: they don't belong to an ITP. They prepare you for *this* moment — Return to Coherence when the floor has gone, Highest Possible Outcome before something that matters.

## The day

Two anchors and an open middle. The ask is small and honest: **listen in the morning, notice in the evening, reach for the rest when you need it.**

**Morning — enter, then ask.** Enter the ITP you're living with. Then, in its afterglow, the one question: *what does my ITP Self want today?* The ITP Self isn't a separate thread — it's what the person becomes attuned to by listening: the possibility of surprise and magic, mind and heart in alignment, their present and future their own to shape. The question follows naturally from the listening; it would feel odd before it. A sentence is enough. Five minutes all told.

**The middle — nothing asked.** Two things available on Today: the ITP's Breathe 11 if you need the body, Return to Coherence if you need the ground. And one thing *arrives*: the Quantum Why, mid-afternoon, as the day's one notification. Today shows it with "Nothing to answer. Just carry it."

**Evening — front and centre, not pushed.** *What shifted today?* is the evening's card, the whole card, not a side offer. And it hands the afternoon back: *"This afternoon it asked why the step was so warm. Did you notice anything?"* The Why plants; the evening harvests; the record keeps it. "Not tonight — that's allowed" is always there, one line, no guilt. Then, if there is something to celebrate, *Celebrate the evidence*; and before sleep, the attunement again if they want it.

Over weeks the record becomes evidence that the Whys were right. That is what the Codex says they're for — curiosity bypassing resistance until the evidence shows up — and it's the quiet engine of the app.

## Sticky, not invasive

Three notifications exist, and only one asks anything:

1. **The Quantum Why** — daily, mid-afternoon, at a time they set. Content, not a nag.
2. **Morning and evening** — gentle, at times they set, on by default (beta testers missed them), one tap to silence.
3. **Your ITP is ready** — when a Personal finishes.

Nothing else. Breathe 11 is never pushed — being told to breathe is exactly the invasiveness we're avoiding.

The pull comes from elsewhere: the ITP being *theirs*; the field being a different place at different hours, so there's a reason to come back; and the record quietly accumulating and occasionally handing something back ("one from earlier"). People return to places that remember them.

## What Today shows

- **"Living with · *The Quiet Yes*"** under the greeting — the organising object. Tapping it goes to the ITP's page, where changing it is a deliberate act.
- The card follows the hour: *enter* → *ask* → (*breathe / return / the Why*) → *what shifted* → *celebrate* → *enter again before sleep*.
- The bar has four places: Today · Return · ITPs · You. Return is a sheet, not a page.
- Your ITPs is a white room with three tabs — Universal · Personal · Primers. No Breathe 11 or Quantum Why appears on its own anywhere; they live on their ITP's page.

## The beginning

First live with *Meet Your ITP Self* for a few days. Then choose a Universal that names your season. Then make your own. The path shows only while new and dissolves once the rhythm has taken hold.

## Open

- Whether the evening is better as words (speak or write) or as one tap on the Why ("noticed something" / "not today") with words optional. Start with words front and centre, watch what people actually do, and let the record grow from taps if sentences don't come.
- When "listening to something else today" should quietly become "living with something else" — probably never automatically; always the person's choice.
